# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-08-28)

### New
- **Translations (beta):** Optionally show word equivalents in another language (from Wiktionary).
- **Synonyms & antonyms (beta):** Optionally show related words under the definition.

### Improved
- **Faster lookups:** Definitions appear sooner; translations and related words can load in right after.
- **Smoother reading:** The double-click bubble stays open when you scroll and follows the highlighted word.
- **Repeat lookups:** Words you've looked up before load faster when translations or synonyms are enabled.

## 0.1.2

- Smarter double-click bubble: meanings grouped by part of speech, expandable preview, and Reading settings in Options.
